# FAQ — emRecupel

### Does the module guarantee compliance?
No. It applies the rules published by Recupel (appliance lists 2026 and 2027,
declarations FAQ 2.13, "declare appliances" and "retailer" pages) and CNC
advice 2009/9 for VAT. Product classification, origin and declared figures
remain your responsibility. Some points are not settled by Recupel (below).

### I sell a PC to a company: separate line or "included"?
"Included" is enough: the company is an end user. The separate line is only
mandatory for an appliance with an all-in fee sold to a **reseller** of
electrical appliances. Set that role on the customer card.

### Why 9.99 and not 10.00?
The line was computed from the amount excl. VAT. Enable "Fee lines priced VAT
included": the line starts from the official amount incl. VAT.

### My customer is in France or in the Netherlands.
No fee is added: the appliance is not placed on the Belgian market. If you
bought it in Belgium (fee paid), it appears in the "Returnable" column for an
"exporter" or "importer" profile.

### A product contains several appliances (luminaire and lamps, kit).
Add one code per type of appliance, with the quantity per sold unit.

### USB stick, supplied charger, cable?
A USB stick, memory or component without its own function is not subject. An
accessory is only subject when sold separately from a subject appliance. Use the
"not subject" status and the search engine on recupel.be.

### Second-hand appliance?
The criterion is the origin. Already placed on the Belgian market: origin
"Belgian second-hand", no fee, the PDF says "Recupel fee included". Imported
second-hand: origin "imported", like a new one.

### I deleted a fee line by mistake.
Recupel tab of the document › "Recalculate".

### The proposal is from 2026 and the invoice from 2027.
The invoice uses the 2027 rate (invoice date). The switch assistant lists the
proposals and orders concerned. A credit note or replacement invoice linked to
an invoice uses the rate of that invoice.

### And Bebat (batteries)?
Not in 2.0.0.

### Points Recupel does not settle (check with Recupel or your adviser)
- Credit notes for a return in Belgium (the module deducts the credit note in
  its own period).
- Amount to show for 2026 stock sold in 2027 by a retailer (the module uses the
  rate at the document date).
- Whether the declaration portal accepts a file import (the module produces a
  screen and a CSV to copy).
